About

Bridging the terahertz gap

I am a physicist working on terahertz (THz) photonics and nonlinear optics. At INRS–EMT in Canada, I develop tunable meta-waveguides for next-generation 6G communication systems.

I earned my Ph.D. in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Guwahati (2020–2025). My thesis, Engineering Broadband Response in Active and Passive Terahertz Metamaterials Using Planar and Multilayer Configurations, tackled the “THz gap”: a frequency window where conventional electronic and photonic devices perform poorly.

My work spans the full device cycle, from numerical design and machine-learning optimisation to cleanroom nanofabrication and spectroscopy. During my Ph.D. I built a THz time-domain spectroscopy (THz-TDS) system from the ground up and integrated phase-change materials such as VO2 into multilayer metamaterials to achieve actively tunable, ultra-broadband THz modulation.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Sc. in Physics at IIT Guwahati, studying the electro-optical properties of bio-nano-hybrid thin films, and a B.Sc. at the Regional Institute of Education, Bhopal.

Research

Research themes

I engineer metamaterials that control terahertz waves: how they are filtered, modulated and routed. The goal is practical components for 6G communication and sensing.

Current · INRS–EMT

Tunable meta-waveguides for 6G communication

Designing reconfigurable terahertz meta-waveguides that use VO2 as the tunable element. The work combines numerical design (CST, COMSOL and machine learning), flexible-substrate fabrication, and experimental validation with THz time-domain spectroscopy.

2022 – 2024

Broadband passive metamaterials

By coupling several resonators in planar metamaterials and stacking sub-wavelength layers, I achieved broadband, polarisation-insensitive responses. These designs provide a framework for low-loss THz filters.

Key papers: J. Appl. Phys. 2024 · J. Lightwave Technol. 2024

2022 – 2025

Actively tunable phase-change metamaterials

Integrating VO2 thin films into multilayer metamaterials gives dynamic, reversible THz modulation over more than 1 THz of bandwidth. This work produced a patent application for an active 6G modulator.

Key papers: Sci. Rep. 2025 · Phys. Scr. 2025

2024 – 2025

Machine-learning-driven design

A machine-learning workflow links geometric parameters to spectral responses. It identifies broadband configurations and predicts resonances, cutting design time from weeks to hours and making results easier to reproduce.

Key paper: J. Lightwave Technol. 2024

2020 – 2025

Toroidal, EIT & light–matter coupling

Collaborative studies of toroidal resonances, electromagnetically induced transparency (EIT) and phonon-polariton coupling. Applications include THz binary logic, biosensing and selective filtering.

Key papers: Sci. Rep. 2024 · Opt. Laser Technol. 2024
